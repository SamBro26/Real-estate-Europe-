# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 17, 2026

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Real Estate Application , named FindMe RealEstate, created by Service Provider. The Application offers a Free Plan (supported by personalized advertisements) and a Premium Plan (Ad-Free, with enhanced privacy protection). This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects certain information automatically when you download and use it.

• For Free Plan Users: This information includes your device's IP address, the pages you visit, and unique device identifiers (like Android Advertising ID) used to display personalized advertisements compliant with EU privacy laws.

• For Premium Plan Users: No advertising identifiers or data for ad-targeting purposes are collected or processed. Your data is limited strictly to what is necessary to provide the core real estate services and manage your subscription.

## 2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)
If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using the personal information described above depends on the personal information concerned and the specific context in which we collect it. We process your data based on:
* **Your Consent:** For displaying personalized or non-personalized advertisements.
* **Legitimate Interests:** To analyze and improve the performance, security, and stability of the Application.
* Performance of a Contract: For Premium Plan users, we process your data to fulfill our contractual obligation to provide an ad-free experience, faster load times, and complete privacy protection as specified in your chosen plan.

## 3. Third-Party Access and Data Sharing
To provide ad monetization for our Free Plan users and improve general application performance, the Application utilizes trusted third-party services. Please note that ad-related third-party SDKs (such as AdMob and AppLovin) are entirely disabled for Premium Plan users, and no data from Premium users is shared with them.
Below are the links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the Application:
* [Google Play Services](https://google.com)

## 4. International Data Transfers
Information collected may be stored and processed in systems outside of the EEA. We ensure that our third-party partners use appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, to protect your data.

## 5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)
If you are a resident of the European Union or EEA, you have the following data protection rights:
* **Right of Access & Rectification:** You can view, update, or correct your profile information and published listings directly within the Application settings at any time.
* **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You have the right to delete your entire account and all associated data permanently. You can execute this instantly by navigating to "My Account" and tapping "Delete Account".
* **Right to Withdraw Consent:** • You can withdraw your consent for data collection or personalized ads at any time through your device settings or profile management.
To exercise any rights that cannot be managed directly through the Application, please contact us through our developer email.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 16 years of age within the European Union/EEA (or under 13 years of age for other regions). If it is discovered that a child has provided personal information, it will be deleted immediately.

## 7. Security
We strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting your information, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 9. Contact Us & Account Deletion
• Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, profile, messages, favorites, and all published listings at any time directly through the app. To do this, go to "My Account" and click "Delete Account". This action is instant and permanent, and cannot be undone.
• Contact Us: If you have any questions or feedback about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our developer support email.
